# Social Pulse — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

At **Social Pulse**, we take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how Social Pulse ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our web application, mobile app, paid advertising management tools, AI services, and social media scheduling platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account & Profile Information
- **Registration Data:** Name, email address, password hash, organization/workspace name, and profile settings.
- **Billing Details:** Subscription tier, billing address, and payment transaction metadata (payment processing is handled securely via Stripe).

### B. Connected Social Media Accounts & Advertising Data
- **OAuth Access Tokens:** When you connect social media platforms (TikTok, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube, Pinterest), we store secure OAuth tokens to publish posts, retrieve analytics, and manage ad campaigns on your behalf.
- **Social Media Content:** Posts, media uploads, draft schedules, comments, direct messages, and social listening keyword queries you submit through Social Pulse.
- **Advertising Performance Metrics:** Campaign performance data (impressions, clicks, spend, conversions, ROAS) pulled via TikTok Ads API and Meta Ads API for reporting.

### C. Automatically Collected Data
- Log files, IP addresses, browser types, device information, operating system, and usage patterns to optimize performance and prevent abuse.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
1. Provide, maintain, and optimize Social Pulse features (scheduling, analytics, social listening, paid advertising).
2. Process and publish your social media posts and execute ad campaigns on connected networks (such as TikTok Ads and Meta Ads).
3. Generate AI copy, images, and avatar video creatives via Gemini and synthetic video engines at your request.
4. Process billing and manage user subscription accounts.
5. Provide customer support and send security alerts or administrative updates.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information. We only share data with third-party service providers necessary to operate Social Pulse:

- **Social Media Networks:** TikTok, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), LinkedIn, X (Twitter), YouTube, Pinterest (solely to execute your requested posts and ad campaigns).
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe (for secure subscription billing).
- **AI & Cloud Infrastructure:** Google Gemini API, Cloudinary / AWS S3 (for secure media asset hosting).

---

## 4. Data Security & Retention

- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3/SSL. Sensitive tokens are stored encrypted in our database.
- **Data Retention:** We retain account and workspace data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your connected accounts, workspace data, or account at any time.

---

## 5. Your Data Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you have the right to:
- Access, review, or update your personal data within your Social Pulse settings.
- Revoke connected social media account permissions at any time via Settings or directly from the social platform (e.g., TikTok Security Settings).
- Request permanent deletion of your Social Pulse account and stored data.
