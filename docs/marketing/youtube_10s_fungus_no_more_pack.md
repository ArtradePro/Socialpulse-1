## 2. Voiceover Synthesis Profile (ElevenLabs / AI Audio)

* **Total Word Count:** 28 words (Paced at ~170 words per minute for high-energy direct-response).
* **Recommended Voice Model:** ElevenLabs `eleven_multilingual_v2`
* **Voice Style:** "Callum" or "Adam" (or South African English male / female athletic voice).
* **Settings:**
  * Stability: `0.45` (Adds energetic inflection)
  * Clarity / Similarity Boost: `0.85`
  * Style Exaggeration: `0.15`
  * Speed: `1.05x`

### Exact Text String for Voiceover:
```
"Stubborn sweat itch that won't quit? Fungus No More washes away gym bacteria and odor, and locks in twenty-four hour skin defense. Love The Skin You're In. Get yours on Takealot — tap Shop Now!"
```
