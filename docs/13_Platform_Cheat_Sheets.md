# 13. Platform Cheat Sheets

Unlock the full potential of each social network with these platform-specific strategies and SocialPulse shortcuts.

---

## 📸 Instagram: The Visual Powerhouse
**Goal**: Visual storytelling and lifestyle branding.

*   **Best Times**: 11 AM - 1 PM (Lunch break) and 7 PM - 9 PM (Evening scroll).
*   **SocialPulse Secret**: Use the **AI Image Generator (Imagen 4.0)** for high-aesthetic backgrounds if you don't have original photography.
*   **The "Winning" Hook**: Focus on the **Promise**. Instagram users want to see the "After" state.
*   **Cheat Tip**: Use the **Built-in Image Editor** to apply consistent filters to all your posts for a "Curated Grid" look.
*   **Hashtags**: Use the **AI Hashtag Generator** to find 3-5 high-volume and 10-15 niche tags.

---

## 👥 Facebook: Community & Conversation
**Goal**: Building loyalty and driving traffic to links.

*   **Best Times**: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday between 9 AM and 2 PM.
*   **SocialPulse Secret**: Use the **RSS Feed Ingestion**. Facebook audiences love sharing long-form news and blog articles.
*   **The "Winning" Hook**: Focus on the **Pain**. Facebook is where people go to discuss shared problems.
*   **Cheat Tip**: Link your **E-commerce Store**. Facebook users are highly receptive to product carousels and direct "Shop Now" links.
*   **Engagement**: Use the **Unified Inbox** to reply to comments within the first hour to boost the post's "Social Momentum."

---

## 🔲 X (Twitter): Real-Time Authority
**Goal**: Viral reach and industry leadership.

*   **Best Times**: Mon-Fri 8 AM - 10 AM.
*   **SocialPulse Secret**: Use **Social Listening**. Set up rules for industry keywords and use **Trend-to-Post** to jump on viral topics before they peak.
*   **The "Winning" Hook**: Short, punchy, and controversial. Use the **AI Reviewer** to score your "Hook" specifically.
*   **Cheat Tip**: Draft **Threads** using the AI Writer. Break complex ideas into 5-7 bite-sized posts to maximize Retweets.
*   **Hashtags**: Stick to 1-2 highly relevant tags. X penalizes hashtag stuffing.

---

## 💼 LinkedIn: Professional Influence
**Goal**: B2B leads and thought leadership.

*   **Best Times**: Tuesday - Thursday 9 AM - 12 PM.
*   **SocialPulse Secret**: Use **AI Guidelines**. Tell the AI your tone is "Professional, Data-Driven, and Executive."
*   **The "Winning" Hook**: Focus on **Proof**. LinkedIn users crave data, case studies, and "I learned X from doing Y" stories.
*   **Cheat Tip**: Use the **Approval Portal**. If you're managing an executive's profile, send them the portal link for a quick "Thumbs up" before the post goes live.

---

## 🎨 Canva: Design Optimization
*   **Pro Tip**: Don't just post raw images. Use the **Canva Integration** in SocialPulse to add "Call to Action" overlays (e.g., "Link in Bio" or "Swipe Up") to every asset.
